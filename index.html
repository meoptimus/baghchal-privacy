Privacy Policy

Bagh Chal (बाघ चाल) for Android · Last updated 31 October 2026

Overview

Bagh Chal ("the app") is an offline board game published by NPG ("we", "us"). The app does not ask you to create an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.

The only third-party service in the app is Google AdMob, which shows advertisements. This page explains what stays on your device, what AdMob may collect, and the choices you have.

Information stored on your device

The app keeps a small amount of data in its private storage on your phone so the game works the way you left it. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it.

DataPurpose
Settings (sound, vibration, language, show legal moves)Remember your preferences
Game statistics (wins, losses, draws per mode)Show the Stats screen

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner ads, occasional interstitial ads between games, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a hint. Ads are served by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested, Google may receive and process information such as:

  • Your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address and approximate (coarse) location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, language and screen size
  • Information about the ads shown and how you interact with them
  • Crash and diagnostic data related to the ads SDK

Google uses this data to serve ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and, where you have allowed it, to personalise ads. We do not receive any of this personal data ourselves; we only see aggregated, anonymous reports.

How Google uses information from apps that use its services is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

If your device is offline, no ads are requested and no data is sent. The game is fully playable without an internet connection.

Your choices

  • Consent form. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, a consent form is shown before ads are loaded. You can change your choice later from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • Limit ad personalisation. In Android Settings → Google → Ads you can delete or reset your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation.
  • Rewarded ads are never shown automatically. They only play when you tap the hint button and accept.

Permissions

The app requests only what it needs:

  • Internet / network state – to load advertisements.
  • Vibration – for optional haptic feedback on moves (can be turned off in Settings).

The app does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, storage or any other sensitive permission.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through an advertisement, please contact Google using the links above, or contact us so we can help.

Data retention and security

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Data processed by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies. Communication between the ads SDK and Google's servers uses encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes to this policy

If we add new features or services that change how data is handled, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
